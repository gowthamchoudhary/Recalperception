WHY RECALL

Text got a second brain years ago. Video never did.

Video was never searchable by default

A thousand personal videos aren't trivial to search like a folder of notes — until every frame and word is actually indexed, not just filed by name.

Confirms who's actually there

Not a guess based on a name mentioned nearby — a real, face-confirmed timeline of where a specific person actually appears on screen.

Trustworthy, not just confident

When there's no real match, it says so — rather than forcing a plausible-sounding answer to a question it can't actually answer.
